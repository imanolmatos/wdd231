Non Profit Membership

Benefits include:

  • Invitations to community meetings and open Chamber assemblies
  • Access to the monthly provincial economic newsletter

COST: Free (DOP 0 / year)

Bronze Membership

Benefits include:

  • All Non-Profit Membership benefits
  • 10% discount on business training, finance, and digital marketing workshops
  • Welcome spotlight mention on the Chamber's official social media channels
  • Access to monthly networking nights with local business owner

COST: DOP 6,000 / year

Silver Membership

Benefits include:

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • 25% discount on event hall rentals and exhibition booths at Azua trade fairs
  • Quarterly featured article about your business in the Chamber newsletter

COST: DOP 15,000 / year

Gold Membership

Benefits include:

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Rotating spotlight advertising on the Chamber website's home page
  • 50% discount and VIP seating at annual business roundtables and galas
  • Official sponsorship visibility at Chamber trainings and major events

COST: DOP 30,000 / year
